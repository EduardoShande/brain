Strategic brief · for Eduardo

Is cybersecurity worth it for you?

You already have software, automation, API and scraping skills. So the real question is not whether to learn cybersecurity in the abstract. It is whether it wins high-ticket clients faster than automation already can.

The verdict

Conditional go. Treat security as a paid layer on top of your automation skills, not a new career. "Become a cybersecurity analyst" is low-ROI for you. "The automation engineer who makes security and compliance provable" is a high-ticket, defensible niche.

Go deeper on automation first

Security is the wrapper. Automation is the engine under every path below. Master it first, then bolt security on top.

06 · Read before you start

The trap most people fall into

Demand stats are not easy hiring

The 514K headline sells bootcamps. The real shortage is senior AI, cloud and zero-trust security. Entry-level "I have Security+, I want a SOC job" is one of the most saturated lanes in tech. "Get certified, apply" means competing on price.

The part most people miss

Security consulting is a trust-and-referral business, not cold outreach. Your fastest first client is a warm intro from a founder or agency owner you already know. Lead with the automation you have credibility in, and let security be the specialization you add.
